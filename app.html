Silo Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts silage with multiplication and division: the volume a bunker holds, the pounds and tons at a density, the days a herd eats through the pile, and the face removal in inches a day. The dimensions, the density, the herd and the feeding rate are yours to measure or read off your own feed sheet. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your bunker and does not know your packing, your moisture, your shrink, your spoilage or your ration, and it gives no verdict that a density is safe or a rate is enough. Every number here is your own entry.

The volume truth

Volume: the length times the width times the pile height.

The tonnage truth

Tonnage: the volume times the density, in pounds and tons.

The days truth

Days: the pounds divided by what the herd eats each day.

The face truth

Face removal: what the herd eats each day, as cubic feet and as inches off the face.
